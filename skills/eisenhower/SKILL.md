# Eisenhower

Turn the user's existing list into an actionable matrix without losing items or confusing urgency with importance.

## Establish the basis

- Use the supplied list and relevant known goals, constraints, commitments, and dependencies. State the time horizon. If current status was not checked, call the result provisional rather than claiming a live audit.
- Importance means contribution to the user's meaningful outcomes or prevention of substantial harm: health, relationships, housing, finances, obligations, and long-term aspirations. Tool-building is not automatically high importance merely because it is interesting. Rest and relationships are not automatically low importance because they do not earn money.
- Urgency means a real time-sensitive consequence of delay, not excitement, guilt, a notification, or another agent finishing. Distinguish verified deadlines, user-reported deadlines, and unknown dates. Do not turn an old 'tomorrow' into a new deadline.

## Score independently

Use I1-5 and U1-5 as transparent judgment aids, not measured facts:

- I5: essential stability or a major current goal; I4: strong contribution; I3: useful supporting work; I2: marginal contribution now; I1: little demonstrated relevance.
- U5: material consequence within a day; U4: material consequence within a week; U3: later known time pressure; U2: no near-term consequence established; U1: no timing pressure established.
- High importance is I4-5. High urgency is U4-5. Explain uncertainty where it could change the quadrant. Lack of evidence is not evidence of no deadline.
- Do not collapse the axes into I+U: that can make a low-value interruption outrank important work. Within each quadrant, order by consequences, deadline, dependencies, and effort where known.

## Four quadrants

| | Urgent | Not urgent |
|---|---|---|
| Important | Q1: Do or address promptly | Q2: Schedule and protect |
| Less important | Q3: Delegate or contain | Q4: Defer or decline |

Delegation is a handling choice, not evidence that an item belongs in Q3. Important work can be delegated too. A quadrant recommendation never dispatches an agent, sends a message, changes a calendar, or deletes a task. Q4 preserves the item unless the user separately authorizes removal.

## Present the result

- Preserve original IDs and account for every item exactly once in the matrix. Bundled workstreams can receive a provisional placement with their deadline-sensitive portion called out. Do not silently equate an entire bundle with its most urgent subtask.
- For each item show its title, I/U, a short reason, and the next handling decision. Keep current owner and already-running or waiting status when known. Do not duplicate work that is already running.
- Empty quadrants are legitimate. Never invent low-value urgent tasks just to fill the matrix.
- Separate overdue physical commitments from optional project reviews. Recommend one next action, subject to the user's current location and constraints, without generating unsolicited baby steps.
- End with the item count and assumptions that would materially change the ranking. No em dash characters.

This skill prioritizes. It does not create new work, perform a full pending audit, rewrite the outbox, or execute the plan unless separately requested.
