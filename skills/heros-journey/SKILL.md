# Hero's journey

Use a story as a reflection tool, never as destiny, diagnosis, a success guarantee, or a demand to suffer. Support the person's agency and ordinary life.

## Ground the current arc

Read the latest user decisions and available current state before interpreting. Scope the arc: financial independence, belonging, a project, or another chosen outcome. Separate reported actions from intentions and assumptions. Do not resurrect old plans or infer completion from an empty task list.

In themain.quest, follow MOBILE_CONTEXT.md and the vault's current CLAUDE.md. Read the live outbox and relevant existing Tasks/ files when selecting work. Archived questbooks are historical, not the active queue. Latest explicit choices override old routing and schedules. Keep private context outside public repositories.

## Interpret without forcing the story

Use Campbell's three broad phases, following the [Joseph Campbell Foundation](https://www.jcf.org/learn/joseph-campbell-heros-journey):

- Departure: leaving a familiar arrangement or beginning a chosen change.
- Initiation: attempts, constraints, learning, help, and developing capacities. The road of trials is an optional motif.
- Return: putting what was learned or gained into ordinary life or sharing a demonstrated benefit.

Say "My read" and give two concrete supporting facts. Identify one unresolved threshold. Motifs can repeat or overlap; no compulsory twelve-stage sequence or percentage through life. A geographical return home does not by itself establish the narrative Return phase. No real person is a villain or owes support.

## Keep major decisions grounded

When comparing moving, staying, or going home, assess the desired outcome, actual total costs, confirmed housing and support, plausible work options, and reversible alternatives. Credit is borrowing capacity, expected money is not received money, events are not job offers, and willingness to work does not predict hiring speed.

Do not use hero language to justify overspending, rejecting family help, burnout, or escalating risk to prove adulthood. A reset or changed route can support the same ambition. Distinguish practical constraints, fatigue, and an unwanted path from fear. Never label rest or declining an option "refusal of the call" automatically.

## Return a short playable reflection

Give the best-supported chapter, evidence, next threshold, and one chosen or eligible action with a visible done condition. If the user is tired and chooses sleep, honor recovery and defer optional life planning. Keep music, love, play, health, and reliable commitments part of a good life. Income and status do not measure human worth.

Invocation alone is read-only. No calendar or outbox mutation, project dispatch, booking, purchase, or personal communication follows from the metaphor. The user performs person-to-person communication.
